## Articles as documents

A single `<article>` directly in `<main>` without a form is a document, not a card: a readable column at `70ch`.

```html
<main>
  <article>
    <header><h1>Release notes</h1><p>What changed in 1.2.</p></header>
    <p>…</p>
  </article>
</main>
```
